GWEmu

Game & Watch Emulator

This runs the unmodified factory firmware from a real Super Mario Bros. or Zelda Game & Watch on an emulated STM32H7B0. No firmware is distributed here — bring a dump from your own unit.

A dump is two files: a 128 KiB internal flash image and a 1 or 4 MiB external flash image. Drop both in, or pick them below. They stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

Or drag the two .bin files anywhere onto this page. See game-and-watch-backup for how to read your unit.
